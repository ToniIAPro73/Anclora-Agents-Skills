# Domain Profile: Architecture & ADRs (Architectural Decision Records)

## Required Structure
- **Title**: Descriptive name of the decision.
- **Status**: Proposed, Accepted, Superceded, Deprecated.
- **Context**: The problem being solved and constraints.
- **Decision**: The chosen solution.
- **Consequences**: What we lose/gain by this decision.

## Red Flags
- Decisions without a "Why" or alternatives considered.
- Stale ADRs marked "Proposed" that are actually in production.
- Inconsistent versioning across decisions.

## Retrieval Pattern
- **Analytical/Historical**: Looking back at why a decision was made.
