# Domain Profile: Market Research & Competitive Intelligence

## Required Structure
- **Competitor Profiles**: Consistent sections (Pricing, Tech Stack, Strategy).
- **Find-Implication-Action**: Every data point must have a "So What?" and a recommendation.
- **Evidence Labeling**: Explicitly distinguish between facts and speculation.

## Red Flags
- Scattered intelligence (one-off emails vs. structured reports).
- Missing dates on competitive pricing or features.
- Generic findings that don't drive decisions.

## Retrieval Pattern
- **Analytical**: Comparing multiple sources to find trends or gaps.
