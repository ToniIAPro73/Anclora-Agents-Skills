# Domain Profile: Runbooks & SRE

## Required Structure
- **Trigger**: What alert or situation starts this runbook.
- **Severity/Impact**: Business impact of the incident.
- **Background**: Why this component exists and how it works.
- **Mitigation**: Immediate steps to stop the bleeding.
- **Escalation**: Who to contact if mitigation fails.
- **Post-mortem**: Links to past incidents.

## Red Flags
- Stale commands or outdated dashboard links.
- Missing owner or last review date.
- Lack of clear "stop" conditions during mitigation.

## Retrieval Pattern
- **Procedural Urgent**: High-speed lookup during incidents.
