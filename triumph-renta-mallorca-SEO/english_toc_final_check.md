1. Strategic Proposal: Web Optimization and GEO Visibility

Prepared for: Triumph Rental Mallorca

Date: February 9, 2026

Consultant: SEO & AI Strategy Specialist

1.1. Introduction

After analyzing the digital presence of Triumph Rental Mallorca, we have identified a critical opportunity: your website features an exceptional product but has technical barriers preventing maximum conversion. 

This proposal focuses on transforming your current website into a high-performance asset through technical SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), ensuring your brand is the first choice both on Google and within new AI search engines.

1.2. Project Objective

The objective is to maximize visibility and direct booking rates through three pillars:

1.1. Usability Overhaul: Fixing the mobile menu and loading speed to prevent the loss of bookings at the final step.

1.2. Multilingual Optimization: Refining content in English and German to capture high-end international tourists.

1.3. GEO Positioning: Structuring information so that ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI systems cite and recommend Triumph Rental Mallorca.

1.3. Operational Requirement

To guarantee the success of this intervention, it is mandatory to have full access to the tool used for creating the website (CMS/Builder). Without this access, the ability to implement speed improvements and technical SEO would be severely compromised.

1.4. Timeline

**Week 1:** Deep usability audit and access configuration.

**Week 2:** Implementation of technical improvements (Menu, Speed, SEO).

**Week 3:** Content restructuring and GEO optimization (AI-ready).

**Week 4:** Results monitoring and delivery of the final report.

1.5. Financial Detail

**Payment Method:** 50% at start and 50% upon completion.

**Validity:** 15 days.

**Ownership:** The client maintains full ownership of all changes made.
